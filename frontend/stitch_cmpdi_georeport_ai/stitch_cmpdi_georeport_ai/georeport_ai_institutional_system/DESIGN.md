## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-density industrial and institutional environment engineered for the Ministry of Coal and Coal India Limited subsidiaries (CMPDI, BCCL, ECL, CCL, and partners). It balances high-stakes governmental accountability with geotechnical rigor, moving decisively away from lightweight consumer SaaS trends toward an unflinching, high-contrast command center aesthetic.

### Aesthetic Paradigm
- **Industrial Precision & Utilitarian Control:** Structural density, dark high-contrast surfaces, razor-sharp technical borders, and clear hierarchical delineations reminiscent of geological telemetry, SCADA terminals, and official intelligence centers.
- **Tone & Mood:** Unyielding reliability, state-level institutional sovereignty, analytical clarity, and audited accuracy. Every component feels weighted, calibrated, and permanent.
- **Target Persona:** Geological surveyors, colliery engineers, data analysts, and senior ministerial secretariat officers navigating complex lithology data, production returns, and high-visibility Parliamentary inquiries.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for sustained low-strain visual monitoring across multi-hour analytical shifts in subterranean command desks or central administrative offices.

### Color Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0F17`):** An ultra-deep obsidian slate foundation providing deep optical separation without the pure-black banding of OLED panels.
- **Surface Elevation (`#131B2E`):** Distinct structural cards and workspace panels with a subtle cyan-slate undertone, ensuring clean containment.
- **Structural Outlines (`#24324D`):** Crisp structural borders delineating sections, tables, and panels with high geometric legibility.
- **Primary Accent (`#F59E0B` / `#D97706`):** Mining Gold / Amber. Dedicated to operative highlights, primary triggers, active folder cues, and critical operational metrics.
- **Secondary Accent (`#10B981`):** GovTech Emerald. Communicates institutional verification, official clearances, system stability, and compliance indices.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#38BDF8`):** Geological Sky. Dedicated to RAG citation anchors, borehole identifiers, and cross-reference indicators.
- **Text Layers:** `#F8FAFC` for unambiguous title and metric legibility; `#94A3B8` and `#64748B` for tertiary metadata, file capacities, and statutory footnotes.

## Typography

The typographic engine uses a dual-font structure: **Inter** handles linguistic density and interface structure, while **JetBrains Mono** governs all quantifications, geological indices, stripping ratios, and documentary citations.

### Operational Principles
- **Dual Engine Partition:** Never render dynamic metrics, volume totals (MT / M.Cu.M), or statutory references in the sans-serif font. All numerical findings require monospaced glyph tabular figures to eliminate horizontal jitter during live telemetry or filter refreshes.
- **High Information Density:** Headline treatments remain tight and structural, utilizing slight negative tracking to preserve horizontal space for multi-column comparison tables and split-panel workspaces.
- **Citation Precision:** Source references and document page citations (`mono-citation`) use uppercase tabular monospaced micro-type to enforce legal defensibility and traceability.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an explicit, split-command model designed for widescreen institutional monitors and field tablets.

### Grid & Workspace Structure
- **Global Distribution:** The core production workspace operates on an asynchronous split panel layout:
  - **Left Rail (30% to 35% default, anchored at 32%):** Persistent project tree, borehole lithology archive, file ingestion queues, and status utilities.
  - **Primary Workspace (65% to 70% default, anchored at 68%):** Dedicated dual-state surface (State A: Report & Production Synthesis; State B: Conversational Parliamentary AI).
- **Density Rhythm:** A compact 4px base increment (`space-xs`) is strictly enforced to support high-density tabular records without vertical sprawling. 
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (>1280px):** Simultaneous split-workspace view active. Side rail locked with independent scroll containment.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Left rail collides into a sliding drawer or collapsable rail (minimizing to 64px icon dock).
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Stacked full-width panels. View toggle shifts into a segmented bottom dock.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, decorative drop-shadows, which dilute data legibility and compromise the technical nature of mining consoles. Instead, depth is established via **tonal layering**, **selective luminescent borders**, and **ambient structural glows**.

### Depth Layers
- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas - `#0B0F17`):** The primary view base. Void of ambient lighting.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Containers - `#131B2E`):** Surfaces sit elevated above the canvas using a crisp structural border (`1px solid #24324D`). No diffuse shadow.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Modal Dialogues - `#1E293B`):** Raised panels and floating command bars utilize high-contrast borders (`1px solid #F59E0B` with `0 0 16px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`) to announce immediate modal capture.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Surface Hover):** Cards and list nodes shift to `#1E293B` on hover with borders accentuating to `#334155`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces an industrial, chisel-edged shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Elements favor small, disciplined edge radiuses (4px to 6px) to echo mechanical instrumentation and tactical displays.

### Radius Assignments
- **Structural Cards, Panels, & Tables:** `4px` (`rounded-sm`). Strictly disciplined, maximizing pixel space for dense tabular mining data.
- **Buttons, Badges, & Input Fields:** `6px` (`rounded-md`). Provides ergonomic affordance while retaining functional precision.
- **Navigation Chips & Query Pills:** `9999px` (`rounded-full`). Dedicated exclusively to conversational context pills, quick-query triggers, and keyword word-cloud entities to distinguish actionable contextual data from rigid structural metrics.

- **Destructive/Critical:** Background `#1C131D`, text `#EF4444`, border `1px solid #7F1D1D`.

### 2. View Toggle Switch (State A / State B)
- Structural container with `#0B0F17` fill and `#24324D` border.
- Segmented pills for **[📊 Report & Analytics]** and **[💬 Parliamentary Q&A]**.
- Active selection is highlighted in `#1E293B` with high-contrast `#F8FAFC` typography and an amber micro-indicator (`border-b-2 border-amber-500`).

### 3. Mining KPI Cards
- Base container: `#131B2E`, 1px border `#24324D`, inner padding `1.25rem`.
- Metric layout: Upper label in `mono-label` muted text (`#94A3B8`). Value in `mono-metric-lg` (`#F8FAFC`).
- Trailing status badge using GovTech Emerald (`bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-400 border border-emerald-500/20`) for positive delta or Amber for operational caution.

### 4. Chat & Citation Stream
- **User Queries:** Right-aligned, background `#1E293B`, text `#F8FAFC`, border `#334155`.
- **Assistant Intel Responses:** Left-aligned, background `#131B2E`, text `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #24324D`. Tables embedded within responses use alternating row colors (`#0B0F17` and `#131B2E`) with JetBrains Mono numbers.
- **Citations / Evidence Tags:** Monospaced pill badge (`mono-citation`), background `#0B0F17`, text `#38BDF8`, border `1px solid #0284C7`. Interactive hover displays popover preview of source document and page number.

### 5. Document Ingestion & Empty States
- **Dropzone:** Dashed border `2px dashed #24324D`, background `#0B0F17/50`. Hover state triggers amber border `#F59E0B`.
- **Empty State Container:** Rendered inside Left Sidebar when active folder contains zero files. Features a centered slate outline icon (`FileQuestion` or `FolderArchive`), title in `#F8FAFC`, and guidance text in `#64748B`.

### 6. Quick Query Chips (Parliamentary Prompts)
- Compact, single-click query capsules (`rounded-full`) positioned above the chat input dock.
- Styling: Background `#131B2E`, border `1px solid #24324D`, text `#94A3B8`, leading mining icon in `#F59E0B`. Hover shifts border to `#F59E0B` and text to `#F8FAFC`.
